      About this build

      Densa Deck v-

      Local deck analysis, simulation, and AI coaching. All analysis runs on your machine; card data and the optional analyst model are downloaded once and never leave your computer.

      How calculations work  ·  Product page  ·  Source

      Not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast. Card data via Scryfall.

      Combo data (Commander Spellbook)

      Local snapshot of community-curated combos used by deck analysis. Free tier — refresh on demand to surface "your deck has these N combo lines" alongside the static numbers. Attribution: Commander Spellbook (MIT, © 2023 Commander-Spellbook).

      Official rulings (optional)

      Wizards' official clarifications for individual cards — "does this still trigger if…". A separate ~5 MB download, not included in card data and never fetched automatically. Free tier. Remove it any time. Attribution: rulings © Wizards of the Coast, via Scryfall.

      (loading...)

      Analyst model (optional, Pro)

      Local LLM for executive summary + cut suggestions. Runs fully offline after download. Choose 0.5B (~400 MB, faster) or 3B (~1.8 GB, better prose).

      AI client integration (MCP)

      Drive Densa Deck from Claude desktop, ulcagent, Cursor, or any AI client that speaks MCP. Free-tier tools (analyze, search, combos, brackets) always work; Pro tools (goldfish, gauntlet, analyst, coach) unlock with your license. The server runs as a local stdio subprocess — no network, no servers.

      Scan from your phone

      Use your phone as the camera and this machine as the brain. Cards you add on the phone land in the same collection and the same scanning session as the desktop Scan tab.

      Sharing runs on 127.0.0.1 only and is reachable solely through Tailscale, so it is never exposed to the local network. It needs a pairing code, is limited to scanning (no deck, licence or purchase actions), and stops when you close Densa Deck.

      Scan this with your phone's camera, or open the link below on a device signed in to your tailnet.

      A live viewfinder is the only thing this unlocks. Typing a card and "Take a photo" work on the phone either way.

      Moxfield blocks direct imports

      Moxfield's Cloudflare gate rejects automated imports. The fastest way to load this deck is the built-in Export → Text feature — about 10 seconds:

      1. Open the deck on moxfield.com —
      2. On the deck page, click More → Export → Text
      3. Copy the list (Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C) and paste it into the Paste a decklist box below.

      Archidekt URL imports work directly — paste those instead if you have a copy of the deck on Archidekt.

      Build a deck

      Uses only the cards in this collection — nothing you don't own, and never more copies than you have. It says what it could not fill rather than handing you a deck with no lands in it.

      Clear my collection

      Every card comes out of every collection on this PC. The collections themselves stay, so your groupings survive. Your decks and wishlist are untouched — they are lists of card names, not of cardboard.

      This cannot be undone, and it syncs: your phone will drop the same cards the next time it connects.

      Retire a group

      These cards have gone. This removes those copies from your collection and cannot be undone. Everything up to this point was a filter; this is not.

      A price records it as a sale, split across the cards by what each is worth, so it lands in your P&L in the same action.
